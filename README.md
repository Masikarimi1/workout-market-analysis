# Workout Market Analysis

A guided DataCamp project: *Data-Driven Product Management: Conducting a Market Analysis*.

## Business question
A fitness studio wants to understand interest in workouts at a global and national level, and decide where to expand its virtual home workout offering.

## Data
Google Trends search-interest data (scale 0–100), 2018–2023, provided by DataCamp:
- Worldwide interest in "workout" over time
- Worldwide interest in "home workout", "gym workout" and "home gym" over time
- Interest in "workout" by country
- Interest in "home workout", "gym workout" and "home gym" by country

The data is not included in this repository.

## Questions and findings
1. **When was global interest in "workout" at its peak?** April 2020 (year: 2020).
2. **Most popular keyword during COVID vs. now?** "home workout" peaked during COVID; "gym workout" is highest in the latest data (March 2023).
3. **Highest workout interest among the US, Australia and Japan?** United States (100), then Australia (77), then Japan (1).
4. **Home workout expansion: Philippines or Malaysia?** Philippines (52) is ahead of Malaysia (47).
